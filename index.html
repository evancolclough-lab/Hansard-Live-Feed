About this page

The live transcript above is reconstructed client-side from StreamText’s public captioning feed for the Nova Scotia House of Assembly, polled every 5 seconds directly from your browser. Nothing is sent to a server — your copy of the transcript lives only in this browser tab (and its local storage, so a refresh won’t lose today’s text).

For the authoritative record, always refer to the official Hansard PDF, published after each sitting by the Nova Scotia Legislature.

No accounts, API keys, or logins are used anywhere on this page — both data sources are public.

Not affiliated with the Nova Scotia House of Assembly. Live captions courtesy of StreamText.net. Source on GitHub.
